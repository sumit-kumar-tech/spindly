Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate Spendly — to display your expense history, generate summaries, and provide filtering. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all associated expense data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use essential infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the app; these providers process data only on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

We do not integrate with advertising networks, analytics brokers, or social media platforms.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the app. We will respond within a reasonable time.
